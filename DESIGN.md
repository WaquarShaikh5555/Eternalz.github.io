# World-builder's Atlas

The portfolio draws from its real beginning in Minecraft servers, plugin setup, and tinkering. It reads like an explorable builder's atlas with live console panels, rather than a generic studio or agency template. Its visual grammar is composed of squared block forms, terrain mapping, coordinate grids, and game-inventory-like facts.

## Color

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page | `#ede6d1` | `#0b1112` |
| Raised surface | `#f5efd9` | `#17201d` |
| Primary text | `#17201b` | `#eef0e5` |
| Secondary text | `#465247` | `#c2ccb7` |
| Forest | `#284337` | `#22392c` |
| Signal green | `#508a2a` | `#a6fa52` |
| Redstone orange | `#c54f2f` | `#ff8158` |

Use the dark obsidian scene for the hero and navigation, light sandstone for the autobiographical story and featured project, and contrasting forest and redstone regions for skills and experience. Signal green identifies selected states and key actions. Use map grids and square edges; avoid generic cards, gradients, decorative blur, and glow effects.

## Typography

- **Display:** Chakra Petch, bold, with a precise, angular silhouette.
- **Body and controls:** IBM Plex Sans, with comfortable reading measure and clear weight steps.
- **Metadata:** IBM Plex Mono, used for short labels and technical details only.

## Composition and components

- Use a two-column player-profile opening, a chronological story with a quick-facts panel, a horizontal skills loadout, a split project showcase, and a connected experience route.
- Keep edges square; use map grids, fine coordinate lines, solid panel frames, and selective offset shadows for structure.
- Let the name, introduction, actions, and existing facts fill the first viewport without card-stack or agency-hero conventions.
- Keep section labels, links, buttons, and form controls legible in both themes and across mobile and desktop widths.
- Retain the existing semantic content, navigation, theme toggle, and contact form behavior.

## Motion and accessibility

Keep the existing progressive enhancements; use motion sparingly and preserve visible content when reduced motion is requested. Maintain keyboard focus visibility, semantic landmarks, clear control states, and readable contrast.
