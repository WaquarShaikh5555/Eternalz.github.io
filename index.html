About

Hey, I’m Waquar

A tech enthusiast who has been exploring the world of technology since 2020. What started as a simple interest in Minecraft slowly turned into something much bigger.

Where it started

Back then I played Minecraft, created servers, configured plugins, set up permissions and experimented with features — just for fun. I was not doing it for money or to build a career. I was simply curious about how things worked behind the scenes.

From curiosity to craft

That curiosity eventually led me to Discord bot development, automation, websites, and now AI-powered development. Over time technology became more than a hobby. I started exploring how things are built, how different systems work together, and how an idea can turn into something people can actually use.

Where I am today

Right now I am focused on learning, building and experimenting with AI, software development, web technologies and automation. I use AI tools to develop faster and bring ideas to life, but I am always interested in understanding what is happening behind the code, not just generating it.

What’s ahead

I am still learning and I have a long way to go — but that is exactly what excites me about this field. There is always something new to explore, something to build, or a problem to solve. My goal is simple: keep learning, keep building, and turn ideas into real products people find useful.

I am not here to pretend I know everything. I am here to see how far curiosity, consistency and the willingness to experiment can take me.
